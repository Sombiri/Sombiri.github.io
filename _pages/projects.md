### ActionCue
{: #actioncue .project__title}

How should uncertainty influence human oversight?
{: .project__question}

ActionCue makes visible how multiple uncertainty conditions are combined into an oversight response. It shows the reasoning behind that response without making the underlying domain decision.

**My contribution:** Research framework, interaction design, and visualization implementation.
{: .project__meta}

### UNACORM
{: #unacorm .project__title}

How can DNA data-storage codecs be compared fairly?
{: .project__question}

UNACORM provides a unified environment for benchmarking DNA storage codecs. It supports reproducible evaluation and reveals trade-offs across performance, reliability, and efficiency.

**My contribution:** Application and UX co-design, experimental evaluation, validation, and coordination of codec implementations.
{: .project__meta}

### DNAsmart
{: #dnasmart .project__title}

How do evaluation criteria change the ranking of DNA data-storage systems?
{: .project__question}

DNAsmart is an interactive visual-analytics tool for comparing DNA data-storage systems using multiple attributes. Users can adjust criteria and their weights to explore how rankings change.

**My contribution:** Research conceptualization, software implementation, visualization design, and expert evaluation.
{: .project__meta}

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csbj.2023.02.016) · [Code](https://github.com/Sombiri/DNAsmart)
